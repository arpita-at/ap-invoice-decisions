AP Invoice Decisions

Invoice in, reasoned decision out

Drop in a vendor invoice. The process reads the PDF, checks it against the approved vendor master and the open purchase orders, and returns a decision with every step it took to get there. Built for Zamp's AI Solutions Analyst case study, PS-1.

Synthetic data. The vendors, purchase orders and sample invoices are invented for this exercise. The files in samples/ are real PDFs and are parsed live by pdf.js, not mocked.
